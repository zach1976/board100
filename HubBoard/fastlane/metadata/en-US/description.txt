Your clipboard, upgraded to a full coaching brain.

Tactics Board gives coaches, players and analysts one professional canvas to draw plays, set formations and animate movements — fifteen sports in one app, each on its own court.

DRAW IT. PLAY IT. SHARE IT.

- Drag players onto an accurate, full-color court for any of fifteen sports
- Draw arrows, lines, and zones — colors and styles your way
- Tap Play to animate movements step by step
- Write a note on each step — it changes as the play runs
- Save unlimited tactics; export as PNG, PDF or video

MATCH DAY

For the team sports — soccer, basketball, volleyball (by set), field hockey, handball, water polo and rugby — set the periods and your starting line-up, run the clock, and tap two players to swap them. The bench sorts by minutes played and Suggest evens out playing time; cards and suspensions where the sport has them. After the match, every player's minutes and substitutions export as a PDF. The set-up is free; running the clock needs Pro.
