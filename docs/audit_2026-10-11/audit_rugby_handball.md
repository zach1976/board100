# Audit: rugby (40 drills) and handball (42 drills), read-only

Every step PNG in `tools/board_png/rugby|handball/` was viewed as a contact sheet, and each finding below was zoomed and checked on the render. Ball geometry was also measured from the drill JSON (scripts and sheets are in this folder: `scripts/`, `sheets/`, `rugby_dump.txt`, `handball_dump.txt`). PNG paths are relative to `tools/board_png/`. Unless a note says otherwise, attack goes up the board. "dy" means metres toward the attacked try line or goal.

## (a) Verdicts

**Rugby: not shippable.** The pitch painter is correct to Law 1 (70 m wide, 100 m between try lines, 22 m in-goals, solid 22s, dashed 10 m lines, 5 m and 15 m lines, 5.6 m posts). The drills are broken in ways a rugby coach will see in seconds.
- **Forward passes.** Almost every open-play pass is drawn going forward. The engine lands the ball where the receiver ends the step, after a run of 11.5 m, so the arrow points forward by 2–18 m. This hits 20 drills.
- **No try is ever scored.** Eight attacking patterns end with the ball flying alone 40–46 m into the in-goal, narrated as "kicks at the posts". The three finishing drills end with the ball short of the line.
- **Set pieces break Laws 18–20.** The lineouts are a scatter of both teams with no two lines. The kick-off lands short of the 10 m line. The scrum ends with a 20 m forward pass into the opposing pack. Kick chasers start offside.
- **Labels don't match the boards.** The pod shapes, the loop, the breakdowns, the kick-chase and the scramble do not show what their names say.
- **Text is wrong in several places.** Thai calls a try "ทราย" (sand) in all 37 rules lines. zh-CN/zh-TW call the cut-out "越位传球" (offside pass) and the maul defence "防守短角球" (defending a short corner). Two to three note lines per drill are left in English in eight locales.

**Handball: closer, but not shippable.** The court is right to IHF Rule 1 (40×20 m, 6 m area from 3 m posts, dashed 9 m, 1 m 7 m line, 15 cm 4 m line), except that the 9 m arcs spill past the sidelines. Most attack and defence boards are coherent and keep field players out of the goal area. The blockers are:
- **Shots.** Shot targets are placed outside the posts, and no shot crosses the goal line. Shots end 1.2–2.4 m out, usually at the keeper's feet.
- **Restarts break the rules.** On the 7 m throw the taker steps over the 7 m line. On the 9 m free throw the pivot is inside the 9 m line. On the throw-off two attackers are in the opponents' half.
- **Goal area.** Field players stand inside the goal area in three drills.
- **Ball goes to the opponent.** Four drills pass the ball to an opponent (or, in the outlet drill, to an empty spot) as if it were a teammate.
- **Wrong shapes and labels.** The 3-2-1 and 4-2 are drawn wrong, and "man to man", "through the pivot", "late runner" and "third wave" don't show their idea.

## (d) Not verified, or verified only partly

- **Rugby formations (R21) and the rugby half-court layout (R22).** No PNG renders them (`board_png` holds drills only, and every rugby drill renders full-pitch). Both findings come from coordinate arithmetic on the code; they are not confirmed on a board.
- **Handball 3-step, 3-second and passive-play rules.** Boards have no time or step count. Nothing shown visibly breaks them, but they cannot be checked.
- **Rugby offside at rucks and mauls (Laws 15–17).** No drill draws a real ruck or maul (see R12, R5), so there is no hindmost foot to measure against. The open-play defensive lines do stay on their side until they run through the attack (R3).
- **Rugby scrum binding** is checked only as shape: 3-4-1 per side, which is correct. Engagement distance can't be judged at figure scale. The scrum's front rows are drawn about 2.5 m apart and never engaged.
- **es/fr/id/ms/vi wording** was checked only for untranslated English, not for terminology quality. CJK and Thai were spot-checked for terms, which found R18 and R19.
- **Rugby and handball strings in `assets/translations/*.json`.** Only sport, formation and marker keys were checked, and they are fine apart from H20.
