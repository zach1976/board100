先修的通病（一处改动修一大片）

  1. 训练说明夹英文句子，所有运动都有，含已上架和刚送审的排球。「器材 / 组织 / 后续」几行只填了中英文，日韩泰西法印马越 8 种语言显示英文。排球 44 个里 31 个、足球 137 个里 117 个。可走在线训练包修，不用发版。
  2. 训练在点题那一拍前结束。题目里那一板没画，对手最后一步走到目标区上。网球、匹克球、沙滩网球、乒乓球、藤球、沙滩足排都有。
  3. 射门 / 得分的球不进门。手球射门停在门前 1.6 米或落在门柱外；水球约 25 个射门停在门前 1.7 到 2 米、和守门员叠在一起；橄榄球从不达阵，球自己飞 40 多米进得分区。
  4. 对拉的球指向接球人的旧位置。斜线对拉画成中路直线（网球 4 个、匹克球 2 个）。
  5. 发球员站进场内。网球、沙滩足排、藤球的发球都有从界内或发球圈外发出的。
  6. 训练缩略图一律画成足球草坪。所有运动的训练卡片都这样，已上架的也是。
  7. 合集版切换运动会覆盖已存的板子（代码已核实：切换时没清当前板子名）。

单项最严重

  • 橄榄球 20 个训练的传球是向前传（已在渲染图核实）。
  • 手球有场上球员站进 6 米区；7 米罚球的人跨过 7 米线才出手。
  • 水球点球从 6 米线游进一段再投；以多打少预设只有 5 打 4；球门线和 2 米线颜色画反（已核实）。
  • 乒乓球双打发球左半区到左半区，同一人连击两次；体能训练把人和标志桶画在球台上。
  • 藤球两个「主攻发球」训练里发球的不是主攻手。
  • 沙滩网球规则写成「落地两次失分」，实际一次落地就失分。
  • 中文把网前类运动和橄榄球的 Finishing 译成「射门」；泰文把橄榄球「达阵」写成「沙子」。

每条发现都由子代理在渲染图或模拟器截图上核实过；我另外抽查了橄榄球传球、手球射门、水球射门三张图和切换运动的代码，均属实。下面是 5 份原始报告全文。原文与证据图在 docs/audit_2026-10-11/ 与 scratchpad。

(a) Verdict per sport

Tennis (38 drills): not releasable as is. The court painter is correct. The drill library breaks tennis rules on the board in about 15 drills: - The four rally drills ("cross-court FH/BH", "down the line", "figure of eight") all hit the ball centre to centre. A player shuffles to a corner only after the ball has gone. - Three serve drills walk the server 1.4 m inside the baseline before the serve. - tn_defend_block_return serves into the wrong box. tn_return_plus_one's first ball also goes into the wrong box, if it is a serve; if not, the drill has the server and returner the wrong way round. - Three plus-one target zones sit in the doubles alley, which is out in a 1v1 drill. - In tn_net_drop_volley the feeder ends up standing on the net. - In serve-and-volley and the poach drill, the return is struck from an empty spot.

Almost every attacking or finishing drill also stops before the shot it is named after. The feeder then walks onto the target zone instead. The "Equipment" line is in English in 8 locales.

Pickleball (35 drills): not releasable as is. The court painter is correct (NVZ 2.13 m). The previously flagged pb_defend_counter crossing is fixed, and no player crosses the net any more. But several drills contradict the rules the app teaches: - "Dink cross-court" and "pull wide" are drawn straight. - The third-shot family opens with a "serve" that lands in the receivers' kitchen, with the receiving team already at the line. - The third-shot game opens with a straight serve. - pb_serve_return_and_in labels both players "R", serves from the baseline, and brings the server in instead of the returner. - The two-bounce drill parks the serving partner at the kitchen. - Speed-up and put-away drills never draw the attacking shot, and they put the defender inside his own kitchen on the target. - The marker_paddle label reads "ラバー" (ja) and "ไม้ปิงปอง" (th), i.e. table-tennis words.

Beach tennis (28 drills): not releasable as is. The court is the right size, but the net is drawn as a faint dashed white line that reads as a centre line. The previously flagged bt_lob_recover crossing is fixed, and there are no crossings. But: - The game rules text says "a second bounce loses it" (zh: 落地两次). In beach tennis any bounce loses the point. - "King of the court" is drawn as 4v4 with all 8 players on the sand. - In bt_lob_over the target zone is in the drilling team's own half, and the lob is never drawn. - "Defending the drop/lob" show the drilled player hitting the drop/lob. - 5 drills give two players the same label (P/P, R/R). - "Smash jump" is translated as "jump serve" in 6 locales. - The return-block receiver sprints to the net to take a serve.

The diagonal-rally and serve placements are otherwise sound (beach tennis serves may go anywhere).

Every step PNG in tools/board_png/rugby|handball/ was viewed as a contact sheet, and each finding below was zoomed and checked on the render. (a) Verdicts

Rugby: not shippable. The pitch painter is correct to Law 1 (70 m wide, 100 m between try lines, 22 m in-goals, solid 22s, dashed 10 m lines, 5 m and 15 m lines, 5.6 m posts). The drills are broken in ways a rugby coach will see in seconds. - Forward passes. Almost every open-play pass is drawn going forward. The engine lands the ball where the receiver ends the step, after a run of 11.5 m, so the arrow points forward by 2–18 m. This hits 20 drills. - No try is ever scored. Eight attacking patterns end with the ball flying alone 40–46 m into the in-goal, narrated as "kicks at the posts". The three finishing drills end with the ball short of the line. - Set pieces break Laws 18–20. The lineouts are a scatter of both teams with no two lines. The kick-off lands short of the 10 m line. The scrum ends with a 20 m forward pass into the opposing pack. Kick chasers start offside. - Labels don't match the boards. The pod shapes, the loop, the breakdowns, the kick-chase and the scramble do not show what their names say. - Text is wrong in several places. Thai calls a try "ทราย" (sand) in all 37 rules lines. zh-CN/zh-TW call the cut-out "越位传球" (offside pass) and the maul defence "防守短角球" (defending a short corner). Two to three note lines per drill are left in English in eight locales.

Handball: closer, but not shippable. The court is right to IHF Rule 1 (40×20 m, 6 m area from 3 m posts, dashed 9 m, 1 m 7 m line, 15 cm 4 m line), except that the 9 m arcs spill past the sidelines. Most attack and defence boards are coherent and keep field players out of the goal area. The blockers are: - Shots. Shot targets are placed outside the posts, and no shot crosses the goal line. Shots end 1.2–2.4 m out, usually at the keeper's feet. - Restarts break the rules. On the 7 m throw the taker steps over the 7 m line. On the 9 m free throw the pivot is inside the 9 m line. On the throw-off two attackers are in the opponents' half. - Goal area. Field players stand inside the goal area in three drills. - Ball goes to the opponent. Four drills pass the ball to an opponent (or, in the outlet drill, to an empty spot) as if it were a teammate. - Wrong shapes and labels. The 3-2-1 and 4-2 are drawn wrong, and "man to man", "through the pivot", "late runner" and "third wave" don't show their idea.

Water polo audit (WaterPoloBoard 2.x), read-only

PNG paths are relative to tools/board_png/waterPolo/. Step -0 is the opening board and -N is the board after beat N. Positions in metres are measured from the attacked goal line, with the figure's feet as the anchor. Every finding below was checked on the PNG; JSON was used only to measure distances.

(a) Verdict

Not ready for a 2.x release.

The pool uses the current 6 m line set (6 m line plus a 5 m penalty mark, as in the 2019+ FINA / World Aquatics rules). But it paints the goal line and the 2 m line in each other's colours.

The drills share three structural faults that show up on many boards:

  1. Shots never reach the goal. Every shot ends 1–2 m out in the water. The keeper has already moved onto the exact target, so the ball is usually hidden behind him. In the gap shots and the centre-forward finishes, the shooter swims after the ball and ends up standing on the keeper.
  2. Defences are wrong. In the "pressing" and "fronting the centre" drills, all five perimeter defenders move behind their attackers on the pool side. In "fronting the centre", the hole defender sits beside the keeper instead of in front of the centre forward. Every defence drill ends with the centre forward scoring unopposed.
  3. Set pieces break the rules. The penalty taker stands on the 6 m line and swims forward before throwing. The goalkeeper outlet drill never gives the keeper the ball. The 6-on-5 formation preset has one player too few on each side.

On top of this: - 21 notes in ja/ko/th contain whole English sentences. - 8 vi-VN coaching texts have no diacritics. - Setup counts contradict drill names (e.g. "Counter-attack 2v1 — Setup: 2 v 2"). - Several drills don't show the skill in their name: catch-and-shoot, drive-and-kick, backhand/sweep, drawing the exclusion.

The 2 m offside rule itself is respected: no attacker without the ball has feet inside 2 m on any step.
