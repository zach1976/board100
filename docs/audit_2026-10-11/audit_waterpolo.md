# Water polo audit (WaterPoloBoard 2.x), read-only

PNG paths are relative to `tools/board_png/waterPolo/`. Step `-0` is the opening board and `-N` is the board after beat N. Positions in metres are measured from the attacked goal line, with the figure's feet as the anchor. Every finding below was checked on the PNG; JSON was used only to measure distances.

## (a) Verdict

**Not ready for a 2.x release.**

The pool uses the current 6 m line set (6 m line plus a 5 m penalty mark, as in the 2019+ FINA / World Aquatics rules). But it paints the goal line and the 2 m line in each other's colours.

The drills share three structural faults that show up on many boards:

1. **Shots never reach the goal.** Every shot ends 1–2 m out in the water. The keeper has already moved onto the exact target, so the ball is usually hidden behind him. In the gap shots and the centre-forward finishes, the shooter swims after the ball and ends up standing on the keeper.
2. **Defences are wrong.** In the "pressing" and "fronting the centre" drills, all five perimeter defenders move behind their attackers on the pool side. In "fronting the centre", the hole defender sits beside the keeper instead of in front of the centre forward. Every defence drill ends with the centre forward scoring unopposed.
3. **Set pieces break the rules.** The penalty taker stands on the 6 m line and swims forward before throwing. The goalkeeper outlet drill never gives the keeper the ball. The 6-on-5 formation preset has one player too few on each side.

On top of this:
- 21 notes in ja/ko/th contain whole English sentences.
- 8 vi-VN coaching texts have no diacritics.
- Setup counts contradict drill names (e.g. "Counter-attack 2v1 — Setup: 2 v 2").
- Several drills don't show the skill in their name: catch-and-shoot, drive-and-kick, backhand/sweep, drawing the exclusion.

The 2 m offside rule itself is respected: no attacker without the ball has feet inside 2 m on any step.

## (d) Not verified, and why

- **Possession clock.** The notes say 30 s. That is correct for the FINA 2019 rules; I could not confirm whether the current World Aquatics edition changed it, because I only had search snippets, not the rulebook text.
- **Exact current marking colours.** The 5 m mark's colour and whether a 5 m line exists across the pool are disputed between sources. WA Appendix 4 was not available to me, so B8 rests on the BUCS/WA-derived listing: white goal and halfway lines, red 2 m, yellow 6 m.
- **Umbrella shape.** Coaches use several umbrella variants, so I did not flag the shape itself, only its passing (M3).
- **Goal height (0.9 m) and the GK two-hands rule.** The board is top-down, so height can't be checked. No text claims field players may use two hands, so nothing is wrong there.
- **Formation presets (B7, m11).** These are not in the rendered PNGs; the findings come from the code.
- **Runtime offsets.** The PNGs came from the app's own painter an hour before the audit. I did not run the app on a device to check for any further runtime offset.
