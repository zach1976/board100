MATCH DAY

Set the periods and your starting line-up, run the clock, and tap two players to swap them. The bench sorts by minutes played and Suggest evens out playing time. Yellow cards, two-minute suspensions and red cards are tracked. After the match, every player's minutes and substitutions export as a PDF. The set-up is free; running the clock needs Pro.
