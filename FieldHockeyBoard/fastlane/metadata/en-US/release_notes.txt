What's New in 2.2.0

MATCH DAY
Set the periods and your starting line-up, mark the goalkeeper, run the clock, and tap two players to swap them. The clock stops itself at the end of a period, with a +1 min for added time. The bench sorts by minutes played, and Suggest evens out playing time without touching the keeper. Green, yellow and red cards: a suspended player leaves the pitch for 2, 5 or 10 minutes and comes back on their own; a shoot-out panel records each attempt. After the match, every player's minutes, cards and substitutions export as a PDF, and finished matches sync with your account. The clock needs Pro; the set-up is free.

PLAYBOOK
Mark your own boards, penalty corners for instance, "In playbook" and flip through them from match day, swiping from one to the next and pressing play.

READY SESSIONS
Six ready sessions, built from the library's own drills: foundation, attacking, defending, set pieces, match week and pre-season. Pick one when you create a practice plan and edit from there.

INDOOR AND HOCKEY5S
Two new pitches beside the outdoor one: the FIH indoor pitch with its side boards and 9 m circle, and the Hockey5s court. Each board remembers its pitch, and the formation list follows it: 2-1-2 and 1-2-2 indoors, 2-2 and 1-2-1 for Hockey5s.

65 DRILLS
The drill library has grown from 36 to 65: penalty corner attack and defence, pressing triggers, the counter-press, playing out of a press, aerial passes, overloads, goalkeeping and six indoor drills, each with coaching notes in your language.

NOTES ON THE BOARD
Write text straight onto the board. A drill's step note sits on the board and changes with each step. Pick a colour or thickness while a line is selected to restyle it.

FIELD HOCKEY BLUE
The app's backgrounds now take the blue of a hockey pitch instead of the dark teal every sport shared.

MADE FOR iPAD
A side bar instead of tabs, the drill library and plans beside their details, and panels that open next to their button. A keyboard works (Space plays, the arrows step, Cmd+Z undoes), and Apple Pencil draws while your fingers move players.

GERMAN AND DUTCH
The app now speaks German and Dutch: every screen, every drill name and every coaching note. That makes 14 languages.

SMALLER FIXES
Turned sideways, the side bar moves away from the Dynamic Island. Back from a board returns to the practice plan you were running. Many labels that were still in English are now translated.
