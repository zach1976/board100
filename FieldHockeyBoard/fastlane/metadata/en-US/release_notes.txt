What's New in 2.2.1

• A bigger court: on a phone the board now runs edge to edge, upright and sideways.
• Sideways on a phone, Back, zoom and fullscreen sit in the side bar, so nothing covers the court.
• Drawing: a line starts exactly where you press, the pen panel steps aside while you draw, and a selected line shows and changes its own style. To delete a line, select it and tap the bin.
• Zoom in and keep working: one finger moves players and draws, two fingers zoom.
• Runs read cleaner: arrows meet their numbered stops, and a pass reaches the receiver.
• The board page wears your sport's colours.
• The Scorer link shows the real scoring app, with its icon.
