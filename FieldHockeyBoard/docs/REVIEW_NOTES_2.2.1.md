Dear App Review team,

Field Hockey Board 2.2.1 is a maintenance release over 2.2.0. There are no new
permissions, no new network endpoints and no new In-App Purchases.

## What changed

1. On a phone the board runs edge to edge, upright and sideways. Turned
   sideways, Back, zoom and fullscreen sit in the side bar, and the board's
   corner buttons no longer cover the court.
2. Drawing: a line starts exactly where the finger goes down; the pen
   panel steps aside while a line is drawn; a selected line shows and
   changes its own style. The eraser button is removed — a line is deleted
   by selecting it and tapping the bin.
3. Zoom mode keeps the board editable: one finger moves players and draws,
   two fingers zoom and pan.
4. Run arrows meet their numbered stops and passes reach the receiver.
5. The board page uses the sport's own colours.
6. The "Scorer" sheet (board → ⋯ → Scorer) shows the companion scoring
   app's App Store icon and name, read from the public iTunes lookup.

## Where to find the In-App Purchases

Unchanged from 2.2.0: Home tab → "Open the board" → the round "⋯" button
in the top-right corner (in the side bar when the phone is sideways) →
"Remove Ads"; or Drills → any drill with a lock → "Unlock with Pro".

## How to see it

Open the app → "Open the board" → turn the phone sideways; tap "Draw" and
draw a line; tap the line to select it. No account is required.

## What was tested

Run on the iOS Simulator (iPhone and iPad) and the automated suite of
over 800 tests.

Thank you for reviewing.
