What's New in 2.2.1

SHOTS GO IN
Every finishing drill ended with the ball well behind the goal: the target was written against the canvas instead of against the pitch. The shot now finishes in the net, where the drill says it does.

THE BOARD KEEPS TO THE PITCH
On iPad, on a Mac and on a phone turned sideways, the players and the ball were placed against the window rather than the pitch, so the whole move sat several metres off the lines. Everything now stands where the lines say, at any size.

ROOM FOR THE BUTTONS
Turned sideways, the menu, the page badge and the zoom buttons sat on the playing surface. The board now starts inside them, and on an iPad the playback and tool bars share one row so the pitch gets the height back.

THE PICTURES TURN WITH THE BOARD
The board on the home screen and the one on a drill are drawn the same way round as the board you open.
