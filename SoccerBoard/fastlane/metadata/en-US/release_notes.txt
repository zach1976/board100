What's New in 2.0.1

Everything in this release is about what the drill boards actually draw.

TELL A RUN FROM A DRIBBLE FROM A PASS
Three actions, three lines: a run is dashed, a dribble waves, a pass is a solid arrow. A player carrying the ball used to get two near-identical arrows side by side.

EVERY ARROW STARTS SOMEWHERE
A short run kept its destination and lost where it came from, so a quarter of the arrows in the library appeared out of thin air.

ONE PLAYER, DRAWN ONCE
Past the first step, a player who had moved was drawn twice — a stride apart, facing two different ways.

SHOTS THAT REACH THE GOAL
A shot used to stop short, on the keeper; the keeper himself stood inside his own net. Both fixed.

ALSO
- Cones stay where they were put when a run goes past them
- The pass arrow arrives with the ball instead of ahead of it
- Players face what they are about to do — a keeper watches the attacker, not his own goal
- A shuttle run is run empty-handed
- Text labels turn with the board and can be set in four faces
- The edit bar offers only what an element can change: equipment has no run and no colour
- The referee and the coach are drawn as people, and turn like people
