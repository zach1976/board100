What's New in 2.2.0

A new look for the board, and a ball that is always where a player would have it.

THE BOARD IS A CARD
The pitch sits on the sport's own dark ground with rounded corners and a soft shadow, instead of running off the edges of the screen. Players, cones and the ball cast contact shadows, the turf has its mowing bands, nets have mesh, and the football goals are pictures rather than line drawings.

CONTROLS REBUILT
A clearer bottom bar with the tools in one capsule and playback in another; larger corner buttons that keep clear of the Dynamic Island; zoom and fullscreen moved into the bar. On iPad in landscape the controls sit under the board so the pitch takes the full width.

THE BALL IS IN FRONT OF THE PLAYER
A player with the ball has it at his feet, ahead of him on the side he is facing — never behind him, never underfoot. A player facing away from you is drawn over the ball, as he would be. The ball no longer hops at the start of a step or lands twice at the end of one.

ROUTES UNDER THE PEOPLE
Run, dribble and pass lines are drawn beneath the figures, start and end at the feet, and arrowheads stop short of whoever they point at instead of sitting on his head.

NOBODY STANDS ON ANYBODY
Every drill in the library — 674 boards — was re-spaced so no two figures overlap at any step.
