Thank you — this is the most useful kind of review, because all three things
you hit were real.

The text tool was the worst of them. It dropped a marker labelled with the
literal letter "T", and the only way to change it was to select the element
and find a small button in the edit bar. Typing is the whole point of that
tool, so it now asks what you want to write before it adds anything, and the
text sits on the pitch as text — sized to the words, up to three lines —
instead of two characters squeezed into a token.

Rotation is in: any element on the board can be turned, so a player added to
the field faces the way you need rather than lying on their side.

Pages are in too. A board is a set of pages now, and you can move between
them to keep several routines in one board.

All three are finished and are working their way through release. I would
rather not promise you a date here, but when it lands, please do give it
another try — and if a short corner routine still fights you, tell us again.
