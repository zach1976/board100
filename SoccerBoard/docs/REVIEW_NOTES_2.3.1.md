# App Review Notes — Soccer Board 2.3.1

Dear App Review team,

Soccer Board 2.3.1 is a maintenance release over 2.3.0: no new permissions, no new network endpoints, no new purchases.

## Where to find the In-App Purchases

Both purchases are on one purchase sheet: "Remove Ads (Yearly)" (soccer_remove_ads_yearly, auto-renewable) and "Remove Ads (Lifetime)" (one-time).

1. Tap "Me" — the last item of the side bar on the left edge of the screen on iPad, or of the tab bar at the bottom on iPhone. (On first launch, tap "Skip" at the top right to leave the intro.)
2. The first section on the Me page is "All drills, no ads". Tap "Remove Ads". The purchase sheet opens with both plans and their localized prices; select one and tap "Unlock Now". "Restore Purchases" is the row right under it, and also on the sheet.

The same sheet also opens from a board's round "⋯" button (top-right corner, menu item "Remove Ads") and from any drill marked with a lock in the Drills tab ("Unlock with Pro"). Nothing restricts the purchases by storefront, device or sign-in, and they work without an account. The Paid Apps Agreement is in effect.

## What changed in 2.3.1

1. Turned sideways on a phone, the side rail was rearranged: the menu, zoom and full screen sit at its head and the board runs up to it.
2. The purchases have their own entry on the Me page ("Remove Ads" and "Restore Purchases"), in addition to the board menu and the locked drills.
3. The purchase sheet is drawn on its own white panel, so the plans, the auto-renewal terms, the links and "Restore Purchases" are fully legible on iPad.
4. New sport icons and redrawn intro screens.
5. After a board is closed, the drill name no longer stays over the list.

## Account

No account is required; Sign in with Apple and Google are optional and only back up a coach's own boards.

Thank you for reviewing.
