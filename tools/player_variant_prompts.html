器械要甩开身体 握在手里、斜向外下方，整支拍／杆／棒从俯视角度看得全，并且不出画。 贴着身体画，36pt 上就只剩一团。

Shade it properly, with its own soft highlights and shadows, but keep every shaded pixel unmistakably magenta: no pink drifting to red, no purple drifting to blue, and no other surface anywhere in the picture carrying even a trace of magenta. - The SKIN of the face, neck, arms and legs is a natural mid tan. - The HAIR is a dark natural brown, close to black. - The BARE FEET are the same natural mid tan as the rest of the skin. No magenta light spills onto the skin, the hair, the shoes or the equipment - no coloured bounce light anywhere. Nothing written anywhere in the image: no text, letters, numbers, watermarks or signatures, and the chest is left clear and unbroken because the app draws the player's number on top of it. No logos, brand marks, sponsor names or club crests. Not a photograph, not flat vector art, not an icon on a tile.
