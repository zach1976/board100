player token · take two

只有球衣跟着队伍走
身上其他地方是它本来的颜色

第一版错了两件事。一是做成了写实运动员 —— 有肱二头肌、有发丝、有鞋带， 画到 36pt 上全是噪点；app 里早就有两个好使的小人，裁判和教练，它们好使是因为 大头、短肢、宽肩、无纹理。二是整个人被染成一个颜色， 所以场上站的是一坨蓝或一坨红，而不是一个穿蓝球衣的人。

要像的

app 现在就在用的两个，风格基准。注意它们身上有几种颜色：黑球衣、肤色的脸和手、白袜黑鞋。

要离开的

第一版。窄、写实、全身灰 —— 染完就是一个单色人。

一张图，两层

队伍颜色是教练选的，不能烤进像素里；但除了球衣，其他地方又必须保留本色。 这两件事只能靠分层解决 —— 而分层不需要你生成两张图。

01
球衣球裤画成纯品红（255,0,255） 带正常明暗，但每一个像素都得是品红；画面里不许有第二处品红。这就是"键色"。
02
其余部位正常配色 皮肤中等小麦色、头发接近黑的深棕、球袜纯白、球鞋白配深色鞋底。不要品红反光溅到皮肤上。
03
我来劈 拿到图后按色相把品红抠出来，自动生成两张对齐的贴图：底板（皮肤头发袜鞋，原色不动） 和球衣板（转灰度，运行时乘队伍色）。同一张图出的两层，天生对齐。

造型上另外四条

04
玩具比例，不是人体比例 头占身高约四分之一，肩宽，四肢短粗。肌肉轮廓全删 —— 胳膊腿是圆棍。
05
肩宽占画面三分之二 v1 只有画面宽度的 54%，塞进 36pt 方框只剩 20pt 宽，比它取代的那个圆点还小。
06
表面全光滑，零纹理 不要织纹、发丝、鞋带、缝线、褶皱。
07
不要接地阴影 v1 自带的那层软阴影，被我拿去盖描边时刷成了脚底一摊白。阴影由 app 自己画。

两段提示词

生成后下载即可，我不会直接替换 —— 先渲一组真实尺寸的对比给你看，你点头再整体换。

Toy proportions and not human ones, which is the whole point: the head is large for the body, about a quarter of the figure's height; the shoulders are broad; the arms and legs are short and thick; the feet are planted wide apart. No anatomical muscle definition anywhere - no biceps, no shoulder caps, no calf shape - the limbs are simple rounded masses. Seen from straight above and very slightly in front, looking down on the top of the head and the shoulders with the chest and the tops of the boots still reading, the way a coach looks down at a tactics board. The figure fills almost the whole square frame with a small even margin, standing upright and centred, its shoulders spanning about two thirds of the frame's width. Arms hang at the sides and clearly clear of the body, so shoulders, arms and torso read as separate masses in the silhouette. Surfaces are smooth and matte and completely untextured: no fabric weave, no individual hair strands, no bootlaces, no stitching, no seams, no folds - at the size this is drawn any of that turns into noise. Lit softly from the upper left by one light. Fully transparent background (alpha), not white and not a colour. No ground, no pitch, no scene, and NO CONTACT SHADOW of any kind beneath the figure - the app draws its own. COLOUR, and this is the part that matters most. The figure is NOT one colour. Every part is coloured as that part really is, EXCEPT the kit, which is painted in a key colour the app replaces at runtime: - The SHIRT and the SHORTS, and nothing else in the image, are pure vivid magenta - fuchsia, RGB 255 0 255. Shade them properly, with their own soft highlights and shadows, but keep every shaded pixel unmistakably magenta: no pink drifting to red, no purple drifting to blue, and no other surface anywhere in the picture carrying even a trace of magenta. - The SKIN of the face, neck, arms and legs is a natural mid tan, clearly warmer and much lighter than the kit. - The HAIR is a dark natural brown, close to black. - The SOCKS are clean white. - The BOOTS are clean white with a dark sole. No magenta light spills onto the skin, the socks or the boots - no coloured bounce light anywhere, the fill light is neutral white. Those four non-kit colours must read clearly apart from each other in brightness as well as in hue, because at the size this is drawn a coach sees masses, not detail: dark hair, light skin, white socks and boots, and the kit between them. Nothing written anywhere in the image: no text, letters, numbers, shirt numbers, watermarks or signatures, and the chest is left clear and unbroken because the app draws the number on top of it. No logos, brand marks, sponsor names or club crests. No ball, no equipment, nothing in the figure's hands. Not a photograph, not flat vector art, not an icon on a tile.
