Needs improving

The app itself is nearly there. Clean design, has most features you'd expect. I just downloaded it but have already given up as when trying to create short corner routines there is no way to rotate icons (for example when adding a person to the field they are on their side and not facing upright) — Also, there isn't an easy way to add multiple pages in order to flick through different routines. Also, when clicking the text icon it is just a round shape with T in it, no easy way to actually type in text to say what you want. Unfortunately, it's already too much of a hassle to use.

文本工具那条当时的提交说明写得很直接：工具丢下去的标记，label 是字面量字符串 'T'；唯一改它的办法是知道要先选中元素、再点编辑栏里一个小按钮才弹出重命名框。 打字本身就是这个工具的全部意义，所以现在改成先问你要写什么，不写就什么也不加。 文本元素在板上也不再是 44 点的圆圈套两个字符，而是按文字长短撑开、最多三行的条 —— 原先两个字符就截断，是因为它套用了球衣号码的长度上限。

发之前必须确认：曲棍球线上还是 1.1.25，2026 年 7 月的包。三处修复都在 2.0.0 里， 而 2.0.0 目前只有足球送审，曲棍球连版本记录都还没建。

回复文案

英文，690 字符，上限 5970。不承诺日期，只说「正在发布流程里」。

Thank you — this is the most useful kind of review, because all three things you hit were real.

The text tool was the worst of them. It dropped a marker labelled with the literal letter "T", and the only way to change it was to select the element and find a small button in the edit bar. Typing is the whole point of that tool, so it now asks what you want to write before it adds anything, and the text sits on the pitch as text — sized to the words, up to three lines — instead of two characters squeezed into a token.

Rotation is in: any element on the board can be turned, so a player added to the field faces the way you need rather than lying on their side.

Pages are in too. A board is a set of pages now, and you can move between them to keep several routines in one board.

All three are finished and are working their way through release. I would rather not promise you a date here, but when it lands, please do give it another try — and if a short corner routine still fights you, tell us again.

— Tactics Board
