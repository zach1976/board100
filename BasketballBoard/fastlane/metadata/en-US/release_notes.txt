What's New in 2.0.2

THE BOARD KEEPS TO THE COURT
On iPad, on a Mac and on a phone turned sideways, the players and the ball were drawn against the window instead of against the court: a layup finished past the end line and the shooter stood on the rim. Everything now sits where the lines say it does, at any size.

TWO BALLS, ONE IN EACH HAND
A player warming up with two balls has one at either side of him, instead of one in front and one behind.

ROOM FOR THE BUTTONS
Turned sideways, the menu, the page badge and the zoom buttons sat on the playing surface. The board now starts inside them.

THE PICTURES TURN WITH THE BOARD
The board on the home screen and the one on a drill are drawn the same way round as the board you open.
