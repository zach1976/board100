What's New in 2.1.0

MATCH DAY
Set the periods and your starting line-up, mark the keeper, run the clock, and tap two players to swap them. The clock stops itself at the end of a period, with a +1 min for added time. The bench sorts by minutes played, and Suggest evens out playing time without touching the keeper. After the match, every player's minutes and substitutions export as a PDF, and finished matches sync with your account. The clock needs Pro; the set-up is free.

PLAYBOOK
Mark your own boards "In playbook" and flip through them from match day, swiping from one to the next and pressing play.

NBA, NCAA AND HIGH-SCHOOL COURTS
Beside FIBA, the field menu offers the NBA court (16 ft key, 23.75 ft arc), the NCAA court (12 ft key, 22′1¾″ arc) and the NFHS high-school court (84 ft, 19′9″ arc, no restricted area).

99 DRILLS
The drill library has grown from 58 to 99: set plays (Flex, Floppy, UCLA, Hammer, Elevator, Chicago, Iverson), Spain pick-and-roll and its reads, inbounds plays, full-court presses and press breaks, zone offence and defence, man-to-man principles, transition, shooting and finishing, each with coaching notes in your language.

READY SESSIONS
Six ready sessions, built from the library's own drills: foundation, attacking, defending, set pieces, match week and pre-season. Pick one when you create a practice plan and edit from there.

NOTES ON THE BOARD
Write text straight onto the board. A drill's step note sits on the board and changes with each step. Pick a colour or thickness while a line is selected to restyle it.

MADE FOR iPAD
A side bar instead of tabs, the drill library and plans beside their details, and panels that open next to their button. A keyboard works (Space plays, the arrows step, Cmd+Z undoes), and Apple Pencil draws while your fingers move players.

GERMAN AND DUTCH
The app now speaks German and Dutch: every screen, every drill name and every coaching note. That makes 14 languages.

SMALLER FIXES
The Pro screen is now titled "All drills, no ads". Back from a board returns to the practice plan you were running. Turned sideways, the side bar moves away from the Dynamic Island. Many labels that were still in English are now translated.
