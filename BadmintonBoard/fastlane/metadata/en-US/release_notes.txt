What's New in 2.1.1

FOOTWORK, CORRECTED
Footwork drills start from the middle of the half court, with the rear cones on the baseline and the front cones by the sidelines. Players no longer turn going to the forehand rear; only the backhand rear corner turns them, and a round-the-head clear stays facing the net.

PRO, EASIER TO FIND
"Remove Ads" and "Restore Purchases" now sit on the Me page, one tap away. The purchase sheet is its own white panel, clear to read on iPad too.

NEW SPORT ICONS AND INTRO
The sport icons and the three intro screens you see on first launch are all redrawn.

SMALLER FIXES
After you close a board, the drill name no longer hangs over the list.
